# Fill-ins: what a short ask leaves out, and where it's already written

Name the job, load what the table says, and let those files fill in the blanks. This page
only **routes**. The details belong to the skills and memories it points to, so check
there and don't copy rules here. If something below disagrees with a skill or a newer
memory, the newer source wins.

Replace the right-hand columns with your own skills, folders and rules. The rows below are
the shape that has worked; the names are placeholders.

## 1. Which job is it?

| They say roughly | Job | Load first |
|---|---|---|
| "make a video", "edit it", "do the usual", "make a reel from this" + footage (a short unique ask goes on top) | **the usual edit**, whatever the person's house style is | your house-edit skill, then your recut pipeline |
| "cut this", "make it 30 s", "trim it" + footage | a plain recut (length or cut only) | your recut skill; add the usual edit only if they say "edit" |
| "add graphics to what I'm saying", "make it premium/animated" | motion-graphics explainer | your motion-graphics skill |
| a codename for a look ("the yellow thing", "Dubai") | that saved look | the skill the word list names |
| "motion video", "brand film", "kinetic text", "3D" | made from scratch | your motion or animation skill |
| "thumbnail", "cover for this" | cover | your thumbnail skill (photo first, from their photo library) |
| "post", "carousel", "ad", "poster" | still design | the client's design system, then your design defaults |
| "story reel about…", "faceless one" | faceless story reel | your story-reel notes |
| "website for X", "sample site" | website sample | your website playbook |
| a zip, "handoff", "sync it in" | handoff sync | unzip at once and verify every file, then follow the pack |
| "voiceover" | VO | your approved voice tool |
| "copy this reel", "how is this made" + reference | teardown / replicate | a reference-teardown skill, then copy the format exactly |
| "find / research / look up X" | research | your research skill |
| "teach me", "what's this called" (camera/edit) | coaching | show, don't tell; no popups |

If it fits nothing here, pick the closest skill from the full list and say which one you
chose in the say-back.

## 2. Who is it for? (when they don't say)

Guess from the subject, then state the guess in the say-back. Write one line per client
or page, for example:

- snack, pack, flavour → **client A** (and its sacred rules)
- AI tools, prompts, chatbots → their **personal AI page**
- stories, history, horror → their **faceless story page**
- agency services, clients, LinkedIn → **the studio's own page**

Then load that page's design system. Client sacred rules sit above everything else.

## 3. Blanks they never need to fill

Each of these already has an answer. Use it, and don't ask about it:

| Blank | Where the answer lives |
|---|---|
| size / aspect | 9:16 at 1080×1920 unless they say landscape, or the job's skill says otherwise |
| look, colours, fonts | the page's design system, then your general design defaults for anything left open |
| captions | their exact words, romanized and never translated; placement per their caption rules |
| logo | their saved logo placement, on every frame |
| safe zones | measured safe zones for the platform |
| music / sound | directed by the video, bed measured under their voice |
| images / video generation | their approved generation tool only |
| where to save | a dated job folder, `YYYY-MM-DD - Name/`, plus any page-specific folder |
| sending it to their phone | a preview small enough for the messaging app, and say the full path |
| which browser profile | their saved default |
| quality bar | audit before delivery; check the effect really landed and the frame count is right |

## 4. Before starting a video job

Re-read the person's lessons file for this job type. The short prompt doesn't switch off
any hard rule, so every saved rule still applies.
